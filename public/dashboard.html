How to read it: Times opened = every open in total (your previews included). Unique devices = how many different phones/browsers opened it — so “opened 6 times · 2 devices” means it was opened 6 times but only from 2 devices (someone came back). Reached the wish = made it all the way through and blew out the candles.

The limit: it counts devices, not people — it can’t promise “her phone” vs. “a friend’s phone,” and it has no exact-time timeline. For a full timeline with dates/times, ask me to set up GoatCounter (free, your account).
